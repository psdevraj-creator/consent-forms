DPS Oncology — at a glance
What the site offers
www.dpsoncology.com

Independent, clinician-led digital resources that make practical oncology information faster to find, easier to interpret and more useful in everyday clinical work and training.

Resource The need it addresses What users can do Access, sources & safeguards
Cancer Drugs Fund (CDF) ViewerNHS England Rapid initial check of whether a CDF treatment may be relevant in clinic or at MDT. Search by drug or indication; view relevant CDF entries at a glance. Free. Current published CDF document; updated after new releases. Scripted QA report available. Confirm final eligibility against official criteria and local processes.
Cancer Research Trial FinderResearch Find potentially relevant recruiting oncology and haematology trials without a prolonged manual search. Search by therapy, phase and recruitment status; view criteria, sites and postcode-based distances. Free. Publicly available trial data. Supports initial identification only; confirm eligibility and availability directly with the recruiting centre.
Systemic Therapy ExplorerData A single starting point for treatment-access and trial information. Search by drug, indication or tumour type; filter CDF options and trials; print or share results. Free. CDF content uses the QA-checked dataset. Trial results are signposting information and require source verification.
Cancer Drug IndexEvidence Quick access to key medicine information, especially for newer systemic anticancer therapies. Review mechanism, licensed indications, trial evidence, dosing, dose modification and adverse-effect information. Uses publicly available eMC SmPC material and summarised BC Cancer Drug Manual information. The SmPC, eMC and local prescribing guidance remain definitive. For healthcare professionals.
PubMed ExplorerLiterature Efficient pathway from a clinical question to a source-linked literature and CPD workflow. Enter questions in plain language; retrieve PubMed results; access source-grounded reads for free articles; save articles and build bibliographies. ✓ Full services now restored — much faster now!Currently free worldwide. Limited AI supports defined search and reading tasks; outputs are cross-checkable against linked articles. Paywalled articles are not summarised.
FRCR Part 1 Study HubEducation Approachable learning support for radiotherapy physics, radiobiology and statistics. GCSE-level and FRCR-level explanations, question practice and mock-paper style material. Core resource free. Supplementary support only; not affiliated with or a substitute for official curriculum, guidance or assessments.
DevStat Statistics AppStatistics Accessible statistical analysis and learning for clinicians and trainees. Guided cases, uploaded-data analysis, statistical outputs and graphs; downloadable offline version. Online data are not stored; offline analysis is local. Users must meet information-governance requirements and avoid inappropriate upload of confidential data.
TNM ViewerStaging Fast staging reference during clinic or MDT discussion. Select a subsite and view TNM information, including TNM 9 and TNM 8 comparison where available. Free. Rapid reference only. Verify against the relevant current official classification and local practice.
